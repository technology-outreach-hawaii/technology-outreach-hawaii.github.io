Celebrating Our Students and Mentors

2026 Orientation

January 2026 · UH Mānoa

Families and students joined us for an orientation to kick off the upcoming session. Students had the chance to meet their mentors, while parents learned what to expect. Setting the stage for a fun, supportive, and engaging web development journey.

TORCH Kickoff

September 2025 · UH Mānoa

ICSpark leaders and mentors—students from the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa came together for the TORCH Kickoff event to prepare for the upcoming session. The team connected, aligned on goals, and got ready to guide the next group of students through hands-on web development.

ICSpark Student Showcase

March 2025 · UH Mānoa

During the Spring Showcase, students proudly presented their projects to mentors, peers, and members of the community. The event highlighted their creativity, technical growth, and progress throughout the program.

ICSpark Winter Workshop

December 2024 · Mililani Library

Mentors and students came together for a hands-on gingerbread house challenge blending creativity and collaboration. The event used building design concepts to introduce core principles of web development and structure.
