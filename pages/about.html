Technology Outreach Hawaii (TORCH) was founded in December 2020 to bridge the digital divide and promote technology education across the Hawaiian Islands. We believe technology is not just a tool, but a foundation for progress, innovation, and opportunity.

As each generation grows more distant from the origins of the computer and the internet, it's essential we pass the torch. To address the challenges of tomorrow, we must empower young people with the skills and resources to not only keep up with technology but to lead with it.
