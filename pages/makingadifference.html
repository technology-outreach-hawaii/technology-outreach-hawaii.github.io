ICSpark

Student-powered, coding mentorship

ICSpark is a group of University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa students who volunteer as program leaders and mentors, guiding 6th–12th graders through hands-on workshops, hackathons, and interactive coding programs.

Part of TORCH’s mission to expand tech education, ICSpark is a student-led Registered Independent Organization.
